# Backyard Barrage progress

Flutter + Flame landscape arena game (winter snowballs / summer water balloons), publisher GameLogic / Ethan. Version `0.1.0+10`. This file tracks leftovers so agents do not drop them.

## Shipped

Playable MVP loop on the gameplay branch:

- **Season switch.** Home, the shop, and the defeat screen use a small season toggle. Winter and summer swap the backyard, kid outerwear poses, projectile (snowball vs water balloon), and impact VFX. Combat rules stay the same. Last season is saved and shared by both modes. The home backdrop follows that season (frost and snow, or sun and grass).
- **Campaign and Arcade.** Home offers both. Campaign (code name `PlayMode.arcade`) wipes skills on defeat, keeps unspent coins, and is ranked by highest wave. Arcade (code name `PlayMode.campaign`) keeps skills and purchases, restarts at wave 1, and is ranked by a lifetime score that never resets. There is a wallet per mode per difficulty (coins, skills, best wave, score) in `backyard_barrage_meta_v1`, document version 3. Version 2 saves keep each mode's coins and skills on Normal and split their per-difficulty bests; an older single-meta save loads as Campaign Normal.
- **Walk poses and rival AI.** Kids use the walk sprite while they move. Each rival prefers a living player kid in their row lane, then lobs with the same lane physics as the player. A rival locks its target and aim point when its windup starts: where that kid stands then, off by up to 0.9 / 0.6 / 0.35 rows on Easy / Normal / Hard (center-weighted; frost kids tighter, rushers looser), so moving during the windup dodges the throw. Easy bots hold about 4.5s at rank 0 and Normal bots hold about 3s, from the unscaled throw-rank charge. Hard bots keep a short windup (about 0.3–0.55s). On Normal they throw about every 3 seconds. After each throw they step one row toward the nearest uncovered player if nobody is within one row. Easy does that check every other throw and waits longer between lobs. Hard throws about every 1–1.5 seconds and steps onto the closest player's exact row. A short lob steps one column closer. A hit steps one column back (or off that row at the back line). They will not stand on a teammate. Higher waves shorten the gap a little and tighten where the lob lands. One player kid is the active thrower.
- **Crew of 1 to 3.** You start with one kid. A wave clears when every rival is KO'd. The count climbs over six waves: 1, 2, 3, then two rounds that stay at 3 while rivals throw faster and step quicker, and the second of those adds 1 HP on top of the Easy/Normal/Hard base. Wave 6 is 4 rivals and wave 7+ is 5, with those buffs kept. Each rival KO pays 4 coins, and clearing the wave still pays `6 + wave * 4` on top. The run ends when every player kid is KO'd. Hearts show remaining HP per kid. Allies still take 3 hits to KO. Rivals take 1 hit on Easy, 2 on Normal, and 3 on Hard, plus the wave's extra HP from wave 5 on. On Hard a rival brushes off the first hit for about a second, goes down and gets back up on the second, and is out on the third. Your kid's first hit is a stun (about 1.4s on Easy, 2.1s on Normal, 1.1s on Hard; no move, no throw); another hit during that stun knocks them out. Rival stun length does not change with difficulty. The active kid has a blue ground ring. A knocked-out kid is slumped, grey, and marked with an X, then fades out after about 2.5 seconds.
- **Skill tree between waves.** Wins pay soft currency. The shop puts Crew, Fight, and Defense across the top. The branches for that tab are a side accordion, and the open branch shows each rank under the one that unlocks it. Aim, Reaction, Charge, and the last two Damage ranks stay grey until the crew has a second kid. An older save that already owns those nodes keeps them and does not gain a kid. The old three-card shop is a skill tree: each branch is a chain, and a node unlocks after its parent. Each rank costs 2.5× the one before, rounded to the nearest 5 (`SkillTree.rankCost`). Purchases, each mode's wallet, the shared season, and each mode's best wave persist with `shared_preferences`. A full tree is 3,437 coins. Twenty waves pay 960 in clear bonuses (wave *n* pays `6 + n * 4`, so wave 1 is 10), so the deep nodes take several hoarded runs. Numbers: Team 20 / 50 (crew 2, then 3). Recovery 20 / 50 / 125 (Normal and Hard, both modes: Patch up +1 and Patch up II +2 HP to every standing kid between waves, then Second wind brings one knocked-out teammate back at 1 HP each wave). Fort 15 / 40 (stages 2 and 3), then packed snow 95 and ice blocks 235 (+4 HP each, on top of stage HP 6 / 10 / 14). Throw 10 / 25 / 65 / 155 / 390 (ranks 1–5, same charge and lob scaling as before). Poise 12 / 30 / 75 / 190 (your stun 82% / 66% / 52% / 40% of the 2.8s lock). Pressure 12 / 30 / 75 / 190 (enemy stun 1.25× / 1.55× / 1.9× / 2.3×). Aim 12 / 30 / 75 (teammate scatter 72% / 48% / 28%). Reaction 12 / 30 / 75 (teammate throw gap 84% / 68% / 52% of Easy). Charge 12 / 30 / 75 (teammate windup 86% / 72% / 58% of the Easy hold, still longer than Hard's short window). Shield 20 / 50 / 125 (1 / 2 / 3 hits blocked per kid per wave, no stun and no damage; no new art, just an opacity flash). Lanes 40 (your snowballs pass your own fort; until this node the current rule stays: Easy and Normal clear a lob that peaks past your fort, Hard can still chip it). Blast 12 / 30 / 75 / 190 (hit radius 1.2× / 1.45× / 1.75× / 2.05× of 22). Damage 25 / 65 / 155 / 390 (your throw counts as 2 hits, then 3; the last two ranks make teammate bots hit for 2, then 3).
- **Forts.** Each side has a fort on cover columns 1 and 2, never the back line. The row is rolled each wave inside the mid band (rows 2–5), so it is never flush with the top or bottom of the yard. Intact `fort_stage_{1,2,3}_draft.png` hides kids standing on those cells. The fort is drawn on the cover columns, and its hit box is under half of that side and about one row tall, so the lanes beside it and the rows above and below stay open. A lob that meets the footprint is blocked unless it is at the top of its arc. On Easy and Normal a lob that peaks past your own fort clears it, so it does not chip. Hard skips that clear, so a full lob thrown from behind your own fort can still chip it. Enemy lobs chip the player fort; player lobs chip the enemy fort (stage 1, refills each wave). On Normal and Easy a player's own lob never chips their fort. Hard turns that friendly chip on. The first hit swaps in `fort_stage_{n}_damaged_draft.png`. At 0 HP the fort shows `fort_collapsed_draft.png`, stops providing cover, and does not block shots from either side until the next wave refills it.
- **Controls.** Each half of the yard is a 4×8 grid (more rows than columns) with a neutral band in the middle. The grid places kids. Snowballs are not locked to a row. The right third of the screen charges the selected kid; release throws. The kid aims straight ahead while power builds. From 85% charge the aim pans at a constant speed (about 12°/s), turning around at the angles that reach the back and front lanes at the rivals' distance, and lingers while the line is on a rival. The release samples that angle, so timing picks the depth and charge picks the distance. The kid stays upright. The sweep swaps five poses in equal bands from the up-screen end of the row to the down-screen end: turn-30l, turn-15l, the existing charge pose, turn-15r, then turn-30r. Player winter draws turn-30l, turn-15l, and the sheet charge mirrored so the kid faces the rivals. Enemy sprites are never mirrored from player art. The aim arrow stays. The left two-thirds are the move box: drag and the selected kid follows the finger inside their half, at the locked rate of six times the old cell walk. Difficulty does not change that speed. They are not snapped to a cell. A tap on a living kid selects them and keeps their feet until the finger moves. A tap on open ground sends them to that spot. The columns and rows still place forts, rivals, and throw lanes. The selected kid has a soft blue glow plus the ground ring. Kids you are not controlling throw and step like Easy rivals. A hit uses a small body circle and a depth window about 40% of one row, so a ball can pass in front of or behind a kid. The snowball image draws behind a kid when its ground track is over that hit box, and stays in front when the track is under it. Kids and snowballs also draw smaller toward the far edge of the yard (75% of the near-edge size) and full size toward the camera. The kid's scale is anchored at the feet. Hit circles ignore that scale. Rivals still take about 1.2 seconds per column. The player's drag does not. Both sides share that ground track: player pace stays about 920 px/s, rivals are faster, and the drawn lob climbs and drops onto that track. A flat throw peaks about 64px above the hand. The hump does not grow with range, and an up-aim stays above a down-aim by the same gap the hit path uses. Hits stay on the ground track. A miss then drops to that depth's ground. Charge is still a half-bell: a tap is about 1/3, about 1 second is half, and a full hold is about 3 seconds. The screen shows a charge glow that grows as the charge builds. The selected kid uses the Studio pickup pose. KO'd kids use the KO pose, grey out, drop, and get a clear X, then fade out after about 2.5 seconds. They cannot be selected or charged. A stunned kid cannot move or throw until the stun ends. An Arcade loss clears the skill tree (crew, fort, throw, and every other node) and the wave, and starts the next try at wave 1. Unspent Arcade coins stay in that wallet, so a saved-up loss can buy a node before retry or on the next run. A Campaign loss keeps bought nodes and Campaign coins, and also restarts at wave 1. Season is shared. Best wave is kept per mode.
- **Round intro.** At the start of each wave, both crews walk in from off-screen to their spots on the existing walk pose. A center banner reads Wave N for that walk-on, then clears when the fight starts. Charge and drag stay locked until everyone has arrived. Pause still works during the walk.
- **Defeat beat.** Before the summary, the existing coin sprite rises with the unspent amount from the moment the crew went down (`Carried over N`). No new art. That amount stays in that mode's wallet.
- **Pause.** Arena Pause button freezes the fight, including the short KO / wave-clear banner timer and the walk-on. Resume continues. Pause also opens Settings or returns to the menu.
- **Settings.** SFX, music, haptics, and Difficulty (Easy / Normal / Hard). Stored in `backyard_barrage_settings_v1`, beside the meta save key. Menu and pause both open it. Normal is the default.
- **Haptics.** Charge release, hit, KO, and a successful purchase. Flutter `HapticFeedback`, no-op when the toggle is off or the platform has no vibrator.
- **UI kit v2 (modern UI pass).** One kit only. Colours, spacing, radii, shadows, and motion live in `BarrageTokens` (a `ThemeExtension`, `lib/ui/barrage_theme.dart`); type is the bundled Fredoka font through the app `TextTheme`. Pills, sheets, chips, toggles, and the wordmark are drawn in Flutter per `docs/UI_MODERN.md`; icons, hearts, coins, and the fort bar use `assets/images/ui_modern/`. Motion uses `flutter_animate` and implicit animations: menu ↔ game fade-through, staggered home entrance, springy button press, modal scrim fade + sheet pop, coin tick-up, heart pop, fort meter ease, animated shop tabs, and a purchase pop/shimmer with a floating coin delta. Reduced motion (`MediaQuery.disableAnimations`) makes all of it instant. The home screen is a responsive landscape layout (compact ~640×360, regular phone, wide tablet) with no whole-page FittedBox. KO / Wave N / wave clear / Crew down banners are screen-space Flutter text published by the game on `bannerListenable`. Hearts, coins, the fort meter, the wave label, Pause, and the charge and move zones are Flutter overlays so they stay screen-sized on the letterboxed yard.
- **Audio.** Studio procedural pack is wired through `flame_audio` (not stubbed): throw whoosh, seasonal impact (snow / wet), hit, KO, win / lose stingers, UI tap, purchase coin, menu loop, `battle_loop_winter.wav` in winter, and `battle_loop_summer.wav` in summer. Toggles gate playback. Music beds were refreshed in place (menu ~7.83s, winter battle ~6.92s, summer battle ~6.67s). Filenames and playback paths are unchanged. All 12 files are present (9 sfx, 3 music).
- **App icon.** The launcher is the blue kid (Studio's `bluekid_final` set, Oct 2026): iOS AppIcon slots (same filenames and `Contents.json`, 1024 has no alpha), Android `ic_launcher` / `ic_launcher_round` mipmaps plus an adaptive icon (`mipmap-anydpi-v26`, foreground + background), and the web favicon and manifest icons. The locked summer|winter clash master (1947595 bytes, SHA-256 `86621c0890c82418a4260b93b956927495c01653ae08034704f5e377d992ff1f`) stays at `assets/images/ui/app_icon_1024.png` and `app_icon_1024_draft.png`, untouched; it is no longer the launcher art.

## Balance numbers

| What | Number |
| --- | --- |
| Rival KO | 4 coins (`MetaState.coinsPerKnockout`) |
| Wave clear bonus | `6 + 4 × wave` coins (`MetaState.coinsForWave`) |
| Skill rank price | ×2.5 per rank, rounded to the nearest 5 (`SkillTree.rankCost`). Chains start at Team 20, Recovery 20, Fort 15, Throw 10, Poise / Pressure / Aim / Reaction / Charge / Blast 12, Shield 20, Lanes 40, Damage 25 |

| Fort cracker | 30 coins, next throw collapses a rival fort it hits |

## Known gaps

- **Dead code left in `lib/` (Oct 2026 test cleanup).** No game code calls `ThrowPhysics.launchVelocity`, `launchToward`, `defaultAim`, or `speedForCharge` (throws go through `planPlayerLob` / `planEnemyLob`), `ArenaGrid.coverCellCount` / `isCoverCell` / `inNeutral`, or `CrewCarry`'s `carries: false` branch. Their tests were removed; the code can go in a cleanup PR.
Document out-of-scope bugs, doc drift, and follow-ups here. Add a row when you notice something you are not fixing in the current PR. Remove or rewrite a row when it is actually fixed.

- **Music loops are AAC.** `menu_loop.m4a` and `battle_loop_winter.m4a` (160 kbps) replace the WAVs: iOS cannot play the delivered Ogg, and the WAVs were 15MB each. AAC adds a few milliseconds of encoder padding, so the loop point may have a tiny gap; listen on device.
- **Second SFX pack is first-pass.** The 17 sounds added 2026-10-08 (`docs/AUDIO_HANDOFF.md`) need an on-device listen. The designer flagged `hound_growl`, `charge_hum`, and `fort_collapse` as possibly too soft or synthetic; by level, the quietest are `armor_block` and `hound_snap` (about 6dB under the rest). The original nine placeholders are unchanged. `CLAUDE.md` still says audio is not shipped.

- **Power-up prices are first-pass.** The seven items use the round icon art (`assets/images/ui/powerups/`). There is no cap on how many of one item a wallet holds; each use spends one. Prices and durations are first-pass. Fort cracker only affects rival forts (only one rival fort exists so far). A Campaign defeat clears items along with skills.
- **River reach is tuned to the current arenas.** `ArenaGrid.playerReachBack/Front` (519 / 398) are 60px short of the bank both winter maps share. A map with a different river needs its own limit. Rivals still stay inside their grid columns.
- **Arena river is scenery only.** Both winter maps (`world/arena_backyard_draft.png`, `world/arena_park_draft.png`, 16:9 centre crops of the owner's 2796×1290 art) have a river in the neutral band. Balls fly over it and nothing interacts with it. `world/backyard_bg_winter_draft.png` is no longer drawn in winter.
- **Rival art gaps.** All three rivals loop four walk frames. The frost kid's contact frames (00, 02) have slightly glossier boots than its matte idle. The ghost walk is a gentle glide and sway made from its idle. Both original rivals charge on their windup; their `aim_*` frames are not drawn. The ghost windup is the v1 frame rescaled to the v2 framing. Rival renders are 512²; the widest frames lose a few edge pixels to the square crop. The ✕ KO mark still draws over the rival KO frames, which have their own dizzy stars. Rival files keep their `_draft` names to avoid a path churn.
- **Hard retune is first-pass (Oct 2026).** Hard's player charge is now 0.7 of the base hold (2.1s at rank 0) and its ally stun 0.4 of the base lock (~1.1s), shorter than Easy's 1.4s because Hard rivals throw every 1–1.5s. Playtest whether Hard is now too soft. Snowballs scale 0.55× (far) to 1.2× (near) by ground-track depth, wider than the kids' 0.75–1.0; the hit radius does not change with the drawn size.
- **Rival perks are first-pass (Oct 2026).** Chances, levels, countdowns, and fort growth need playtesting. Quick hands shows the Power throw bolt; there is no art of its own. Rival Big splat throws hit kids nearby for one heart, without a middle bonus. Extra rival forts have no HUD meter.
- **Wave report and per-kid skills are first-pass (Oct 2026).** Revive (100, doubling per kid) and heal (20) prices, the 60% personal price, and Health ranks need playtesting. `RunLedger.kidLosses` is no longer used (kids do not leave the crew) but still reads from saves. The wave report has no motion polish yet.
- **Team art, shield, and bunker buster are first-pass (Oct 2026).** The iron shield only shows on standing frames (idle / pickup): the run, aim, and throw frames have no hand offsets yet, so it hides then. Rivals use the frost kid's offsets, so on the ghost and rusher it sits approximately. Bosses never show it. Unused team frames (`aim_00_profile`, flipped runs, `aim_away_01_alt`) and the 1024 masters were not copied.

- **Report and menu layout is first-pass (Oct 2026).** The kid portraits are each kid's idle and KO art zoomed in, not head-and-shoulders portraits. - **Ads are live on iOS only.** iOS uses the live AdMob app id (`ca-app-pub-7671007992790429~3358046608`, Info.plist and `AdConfig.iosAppId`). Ad units switch on build type: debug and profile use Google's test interstitial; release uses the live interstitial unit `AdConfig.iosInterstitialLiveId` (`ca-app-pub-7671007992790429/2173544178`). AdMob serving is limited until the app is live and linked to its App Store listing, and `app-ads.txt` is not set up on the developer site yet. Interstitials aim at about one every five waves. Every ad needs at least one won wave and 3 minutes of fighting since the last ad (or since the app opened), so losing wave 1 over and over never shows one and a new player gets a few minutes first. Past that, one shows after every boss wave clear and every loss (after the coin beat), and otherwise at a wave clear or Pause → menu once 5 waves have been won since the last ad. Never in a fight. Only fight time counts; a missed show resets nothing; the counts reset with the app. The numbers (`AdConfig.interstitialWaves`, `interstitialMinGap`) are first-pass. Remove Ads is the non-consumable `dev.gamelogic.backyardbarrage.removeads` (created in App Store Connect; price set there). A refund does not clear the on-device entitlement. Android still uses Google's sample app id and unit; Play Billing is not set up. Ads are kid-safe (general audience, treated as directed to children): no iOS tracking prompt (the ATT plugin and `NSUserTrackingUsageDescription` are gone), the privacy manifest declares no tracking, every request has child age treatment, a G content ceiling, and is non-personalised, and consent is re-checked before every load. Remove Ads and Restore sit behind a grown-up check (a times-table question); a double tap starts one purchase. The App Store privacy label must match (no "Data Used to Track You").
