# Backyard Barrage progress

Flutter + Flame landscape arena game (winter snowballs / summer water balloons), publisher GameLogic / Ethan. Version `0.1.0+10`. This file tracks leftovers so agents do not drop them.

## Shipped

Playable MVP loop on the gameplay branch:

- **Season switch.** Home, the shop, and the defeat screen use a small season toggle. Winter and summer swap the backyard, kid outerwear poses, projectile (snowball vs water balloon), and impact VFX. Combat rules stay the same. Last season is saved and shared by both modes. The home backdrop follows that season (frost and snow, or sun and grass).
- **Campaign and Arcade.** Home offers both. Campaign (code name `PlayMode.arcade`) wipes skills on defeat, keeps unspent coins, and is ranked by highest wave. Arcade (code name `PlayMode.campaign`) keeps skills and purchases, restarts at wave 1, and is ranked by a lifetime score that never resets. There is a wallet per mode per difficulty (coins, skills, best wave, score) in `backyard_barrage_meta_v1`, document version 3. Version 2 saves keep each mode's coins and skills on Normal and split their per-difficulty bests; an older single-meta save loads as Campaign Normal.
- **Walk poses and rival AI.** Kids use the walk sprite while they move. Each rival prefers a living player kid in their row lane, then lobs with the same lane physics as the player. Easy bots hold about 4.5s at rank 0 and Normal bots hold about 3s, from the unscaled throw-rank charge. Hard bots keep a short windup (about 0.3–0.55s). On Normal they throw about every 3 seconds. After each throw they step one row toward the nearest uncovered player if nobody is within one row. Easy does that check every other throw and waits longer between lobs. Hard throws about every 1–1.5 seconds and steps onto the closest player's exact row. A short lob steps one column closer. A hit steps one column back (or off that row at the back line). They will not stand on a teammate. Higher waves shorten the gap a little and tighten where the lob lands. One player kid is the active thrower.
- **Crew of 1 to 3.** You start with one kid. A wave clears when every rival is KO'd. The count climbs over six waves: 1, 2, 3, then two rounds that stay at 3 while rivals throw faster and step quicker, and the second of those adds 1 HP on top of the Easy/Normal/Hard base. Wave 6 is 4 rivals and wave 7+ is 5, with those buffs kept. Each rival KO pays 8 coins, and clearing the wave still pays `12 + wave * 8` on top. The run ends when every player kid is KO'd. Hearts show remaining HP per kid. Allies still take 3 hits to KO. Rivals take 1 hit on Easy, 2 on Normal, and 3 on Hard, plus the wave's extra HP from wave 5 on. On Hard a rival brushes off the first hit for about a second, goes down and gets back up on the second, and is out on the third. Your kid's first hit is a stun (about 2.8 seconds on Hard, half on Easy, three quarters on Normal; no move, no throw); another hit during that stun knocks them out. Rival stun length does not change with difficulty. The active kid has a blue ground ring. A knocked-out kid is slumped, grey, and marked with an X, then fades out after about 2.5 seconds.
- **Skill tree between waves.** Wins pay soft currency. The shop puts Crew, Fight, and Defense across the top. The branches for that tab are a side accordion, and the open branch shows each rank under the one that unlocks it. Aim, Reaction, Charge, and the last two Damage ranks stay grey until the crew has a second kid. An older save that already owns those nodes keeps them and does not gain a kid. The old three-card shop is a skill tree: each branch is a chain, and a node unlocks after its parent. Costs rise along the chain. Purchases, each mode's wallet, the shared season, and each mode's best wave persist with `shared_preferences`. A full tree is 2620 coins. Twenty waves pay 1920 (wave *n* pays `12 + n * 8`, so wave 1 is 20), so the deep nodes take more than one hoarded run. Numbers: Team 18 / 55 (crew 2, then 3). Fort 22 / 60 (stages 2 and 3), then packed snow 90 and ice blocks 130 (+4 HP each, on top of stage HP 6 / 10 / 14). Throw 16 / 34 / 58 / 88 / 124 (ranks 1–5, same charge and lob scaling as before). Poise 20 / 42 / 70 / 105 (your stun 82% / 66% / 52% / 40% of the 2.8s lock). Pressure 20 / 42 / 70 / 105 (enemy stun 1.25× / 1.55× / 1.9× / 2.3×). Aim 22 / 46 / 78 (teammate scatter 72% / 48% / 28%). Reaction 22 / 46 / 78 (teammate throw gap 84% / 68% / 52% of Easy). Charge 22 / 46 / 78 (teammate windup 86% / 72% / 58% of the Easy hold, still longer than Hard's short window). Shield 36 / 72 / 120 (1 / 2 / 3 hits blocked per kid per wave, no stun and no damage; no new art, just an opacity flash). Lanes 48 (your snowballs pass your own fort; until this node the current rule stays: Easy and Normal clear a lob that peaks past your fort, Hard can still chip it). Blast 24 / 48 / 80 / 120 (hit radius 1.2× / 1.45× / 1.75× / 2.05× of 22). Damage 40 / 85 / 140 / 200 (your throw counts as 2 hits, then 3; the last two ranks make teammate bots hit for 2, then 3).
- **Forts.** Each side has a fort on cover columns 1 and 2, never the back line. The row is rolled each wave inside the mid band (rows 2–5), so it is never flush with the top or bottom of the yard. Intact `fort_stage_{1,2,3}_draft.png` hides kids standing on those cells. The fort is drawn on the cover columns, and its hit box is under half of that side and about one row tall, so the lanes beside it and the rows above and below stay open. A lob that meets the footprint is blocked unless it is at the top of its arc. On Easy and Normal a lob that peaks past your own fort clears it, so it does not chip. Hard skips that clear, so a full lob thrown from behind your own fort can still chip it. Enemy lobs chip the player fort; player lobs chip the enemy fort (stage 1, refills each wave). On Normal and Easy a player's own lob never chips their fort. Hard turns that friendly chip on. The first hit swaps in `fort_stage_{n}_damaged_draft.png`. At 0 HP the fort shows `fort_collapsed_draft.png`, stops providing cover, and does not block shots from either side until the next wave refills it.
- **Controls.** Each half of the yard is a 4×8 grid (more rows than columns) with a neutral band in the middle. The grid places kids. Snowballs are not locked to a row. The right third of the screen charges the selected kid; release throws. While the hold lasts the aim sweeps ±20° over about 3.6 seconds (one third the old speed), and the release samples that angle, so timing picks the depth and charge picks the distance. The kid stays upright. The sweep swaps five poses in equal bands from the up-screen end of the row to the down-screen end: turn-30l, turn-15l, the existing charge pose, turn-15r, then turn-30r. Player winter draws turn-30l, turn-15l, and the sheet charge mirrored so the kid faces the rivals. Enemy sprites are never mirrored from player art. The aim arrow stays. The left two-thirds are the move box: drag and the selected kid follows the finger inside their half, at the locked rate of six times the old cell walk. Difficulty does not change that speed. They are not snapped to a cell. A tap on a living kid selects them and keeps their feet until the finger moves. A tap on open ground sends them to that spot. The columns and rows still place forts, rivals, and throw lanes. The selected kid has a soft blue glow plus the ground ring. Kids you are not controlling throw and step like Easy rivals. A hit uses a small body circle and a depth window about 40% of one row, so a ball can pass in front of or behind a kid. The snowball image draws behind a kid when its ground track is over that hit box, and stays in front when the track is under it. Kids and snowballs also draw smaller toward the far edge of the yard (75% of the near-edge size) and full size toward the camera. The kid's scale is anchored at the feet. Hit circles ignore that scale. Rivals still take about 1.2 seconds per column. The player's drag does not. Both sides share that ground track: player pace stays about 920 px/s, rivals are faster, and the drawn lob climbs and drops onto that track. A flat throw peaks about 64px above the hand. The hump does not grow with range, and an up-aim stays above a down-aim by the same gap the hit path uses. Hits stay on the ground track. A miss then drops to that depth's ground. Charge is still a half-bell: a tap is about 1/3, about 1 second is half, and a full hold is about 3 seconds. The screen shows a charge glow that grows as the charge builds. The selected kid uses the Studio pickup pose. KO'd kids use the KO pose, grey out, drop, and get a clear X, then fade out after about 2.5 seconds. They cannot be selected or charged. A stunned kid cannot move or throw until the stun ends. An Arcade loss clears the skill tree (crew, fort, throw, and every other node) and the wave, and starts the next try at wave 1. Unspent Arcade coins stay in that wallet, so a saved-up loss can buy a node before retry or on the next run. A Campaign loss keeps bought nodes and Campaign coins, and also restarts at wave 1. Season is shared. Best wave is kept per mode.
- **Round intro.** At the start of each wave, both crews walk in from off-screen to their spots on the existing walk pose. A center banner reads Wave N for that walk-on, then clears when the fight starts. Charge and drag stay locked until everyone has arrived. Pause still works during the walk.
- **Defeat beat.** Before the summary, the existing coin sprite rises with the unspent amount from the moment the crew went down (`Carried over N`). No new art. That amount stays in that mode's wallet.
- **Pause.** Arena Pause button freezes the fight, including the short KO / wave-clear banner timer and the walk-on. Resume continues. Pause also opens Settings or returns to the menu.
- **Settings.** SFX, music, haptics, and Difficulty (Easy / Normal / Hard). Stored in `backyard_barrage_settings_v1`, beside the meta save key. Menu and pause both open it. Normal is the default.
- **Haptics.** Charge release, hit, KO, and a successful purchase. Flutter `HapticFeedback`, no-op when the toggle is off or the platform has no vibrator.
- **UI kit v2 (modern UI pass).** One kit only. Colours, spacing, radii, shadows, and motion live in `BarrageTokens` (a `ThemeExtension`, `lib/ui/barrage_theme.dart`); type is the bundled Fredoka font through the app `TextTheme`. Pills, sheets, chips, toggles, and the wordmark are drawn in Flutter per `docs/UI_MODERN.md`; icons, hearts, coins, and the fort bar use `assets/images/ui_modern/`. Motion uses `flutter_animate` and implicit animations: menu ↔ game fade-through, staggered home entrance, springy button press, modal scrim fade + sheet pop, coin tick-up, heart pop, fort meter ease, animated shop tabs, and a purchase pop/shimmer with a floating coin delta. Reduced motion (`MediaQuery.disableAnimations`) makes all of it instant. The home screen is a responsive landscape layout (compact ~640×360, regular phone, wide tablet) with no whole-page FittedBox. KO / Wave N / wave clear / Crew down banners are screen-space Flutter text published by the game on `bannerListenable`. Hearts, coins, the fort meter, the wave label, Pause, and the charge and move zones are Flutter overlays so they stay screen-sized on the letterboxed yard.
- **Audio.** Studio procedural pack is wired through `flame_audio` (not stubbed): throw whoosh, seasonal impact (snow / wet), hit, KO, win / lose stingers, UI tap, purchase coin, menu loop, `battle_loop_winter.wav` in winter, and `battle_loop_summer.wav` in summer. Toggles gate playback. Music beds were refreshed in place (menu ~7.83s, winter battle ~6.92s, summer battle ~6.67s). Filenames and playback paths are unchanged. All 12 files are present (9 sfx, 3 music).
- **App icon.** Locked summer|winter clash master (1947595 bytes, SHA-256 `86621c0890c82418a4260b93b956927495c01653ae08034704f5e377d992ff1f`) at `assets/images/ui/app_icon_1024.png` and `app_icon_1024_draft.png`. Those bytes stay. Launcher slots (iOS AppIcon, Android `ic_launcher` mipmaps, macOS AppIcon) are resizes of `assets/images/ui/app_icon_1024_fill.png`, the same clash with the navy side bars cropped out. The 1024 platform slots are that fill file's bytes.

## Known gaps

Document out-of-scope bugs, doc drift, and follow-ups here. Add a row when you notice something you are not fixing in the current PR. Remove or rewrite a row when it is actually fixed.

- **Arena river is scenery only.** Both winter maps (`world/arena_backyard_draft.png`, `world/arena_park_draft.png`, 16:9 centre crops of the owner's 2796×1290 art) have a river in the neutral band. Balls fly over it and nothing interacts with it. `world/backyard_bg_winter_draft.png` is no longer drawn in winter.
- **Rival art gaps.** The rusher is a stand-in on the original 2D rival kid. Both rivals charge on their windup; their `aim_*` frames (a smoother sculpt) are not drawn. The ghost windup is the v1 frame from `characters/enemy/ghost/` rescaled to the v2 framing, since v2 has none. The throw frames have the ball drawn leaving the hand, so for about a quarter second it shows beside the real projectile (the raw README suggests hiding one). Rival renders are 512²; the widest frames lose a few edge pixels to the square crop. The ✕ KO mark still draws over the rival KO frames, which have their own dizzy stars. Rivals have no walk frames (idle stands in).
- **Rival type numbers are first-pass.** `RivalProfile` (gap, windup, scatter, step, HP) and `RivalRoster` unlock waves need playtesting per difficulty.
- **Summer is switched off.** `Season.playable` is winter-only until summer art matches the 3D winter pack. Saves on summer load as winter. Summer assets, `SeasonToggle`, and the summer kit load in the game are kept so adding `Season.summer` back to `Season.playable` restores the toggles. The store one-liner and README still mention water balloons.
- **Old saves land on Normal.** Before wallets split by difficulty, each mode had one wallet; its coins and skills move to Normal and Easy/Hard start fresh. A lone old best wave (no difficulty recorded) counts as Normal. Old saves have no score, so Arcade scores start at 0.
- **Mode code names are swapped from the shown names.** `PlayMode.arcade` shows as Campaign and `PlayMode.campaign` as Arcade, because saves store the code names. Rename with a save migration if it causes confusion.
- **Quitting a Campaign run from Pause keeps the skills.** Skills only wipe when the crew goes down, so a player can quit before losing to protect them.
- **Player winter is Ethan's 3D kid, with gaps.** The game draws the six 512 frames from `characters/player/ethan3d/` (listed one by one in `pubspec.yaml`, so the 1024s and review sheets stay out of the bundle). Aiming up-screen holds the profile because there are no turned-away frames. There are no approved idle, throw, hit, or KO frames, so those reuse `aim_01` (throw adds the lunge, KO tips the kid over). The run cycle is two frames. The older Studio 3D files (`player_*_winter_3d_*`) are no longer drawn.
- **Summer player art is still the 2D drafts.** Only winter uses Ethan's 3D kid.
- **HQ turn-yaw regenerator is not in the repo.** `docs/TURN_YAWS.md` names `scripts/generate_turn_yaws.py` as the writer of the 16 turn drafts, the 4 HQ charge drafts, and `turn_yaw_sheet_hq.png`. Studio delivered those binaries. `scripts/generate_poses_polish.py` still paints `charge` and would overwrite the HQ charge drafts if re-run. The pose scripts do not redraw the yaws.
- **Enemy fort has no HUD meter.** The enemy fort uses the stage-1 art and shows damage on the sprite. Hearts, coins, and the player fort bar are the screen-space meters.
- **Old flightVisualY hump is still unused.** Live snowballs draw `loftAt` on the ground track (a 64px climb and drop). `flightVisualY` / `_visualHump` still peaks around 40–120px, but current lobs set `groundTrack` and do not draw that path.
- **Game overlays cut out instead of animating out.** Pause, settings (in a match), shop, and defeat pop in with a scrim fade and sheet spring, but `overlays.remove` drops them on the same frame. Only the home-screen settings sheet and the menu ↔ game swap animate out. An exit would need the game to defer `overlays.remove`, which this UI pass did not touch.
- **Center banners leave on a cut.** KO, Wave N, wave clear, and Crew down are now screen-space Flutter text with an animated entrance (`lib/ui/match_banner.dart`), but they disappear the moment the game clears them so the banner timing stays exactly as it was.
- **Rivals are a little more accurate.** They now aim at a kid's real depth instead of the nearest lane, and the hit window covers a full row. Standing between two rows no longer gives free misses (about one in five long rival throws used to pass through). Retune `DifficultyTuning` if Normal now feels hard.
- **Aim friction and assist are first-pass numbers.** `ThrowPhysics.aimFriction` (0.35), `aimAssistPx` (14), and `maxAimSlope` (0.2) need on-device playtest.
- **Aim friction and release assist apply on every difficulty.** Normal hides the target ring and Hard hides the floor preview, but the sweep still slows on a rival and a near miss is still nudged on all three. Turn them down on Hard if it should be pure timing.
- **Hit feel has no in-game motion toggle.** Hit-stop and screen shake follow the OS reduce-motion setting only. Hit and impact sounds play at one pitch (no per-hit variation). On the last rival of a wave, the "+8" coin pop rises behind the KO banner.
- **Rivals still step on the yard grid.** The player drags freely inside their half. Rival lane steps, fort cover columns, and throw-lane rows still use the 4×8 cells.
- **Locked app icon master still has navy side bars.** `assets/images/ui/app_icon_1024.png` (SHA-256 `86621c0890c82418a4260b93b956927495c01653ae08034704f5e377d992ff1f`) is unchanged on purpose. Launchers use `app_icon_1024_fill.png`. Do not overwrite the locked master.
- **Classic wood kit PNGs are unused.** The classic kit and the Settings → Modern UI toggle are gone (old saves with `modernUi` still load; the field is ignored). Everything under `assets/images/ui/` except the app icons is no longer loaded by Flutter screens and could be deleted later. The Flame coin-carry beat still loads `ui/coin_draft.png` in `backyard_barrage_game.dart` and was left alone.
- **Some v2 kit PNGs are now drawn in Flutter instead.** Buttons, the modal sheet, HUD chips, toggles, season chips, the shop card, and the wordmark follow the `docs/UI_MODERN.md` shape rules in code (crisp stadiums and radii at any size). `btn_*_v2`, `panel_modal_v2`, `hud_chip_bg_v2`, `toggle_*_v2`, `chip_season_*_v2`, `shop_card_v2`, and `wordmark_backyard_barrage_v2` stay on disk unused. Icons, hearts, coin, and the fort bar still use the v2 PNGs.
- **Fredoka ships as three static weights.** `assets/fonts/fredoka/` holds Medium, SemiBold, and Bold cut from the google/fonts variable TTF with fontTools (OFL 1.1, `OFL.txt` beside them). Regular/Light are not bundled; a 400 request renders Medium.
- **UI motion is off in widget tests by default.** `test/flutter_test_config.dart` sets `BarrageMotion.debugDisable` because `flutter_animate` starts every animation on a zero-length timer. `test/motion_test.dart` and one shop test turn it on and pump through.
- **App icon still lives under the classic UI folder.** `assets/images/ui/app_icon_1024.png` is the launcher icon. Do not delete `assets/images/ui/` while it is there.
- **Props folders are empty.** `assets/images/props/winter/` and `props/summer/` exist so the asset list analyzes, but they have no sprites yet.
- **App icon is unused inside the game UI.** The clash icon is the store/launcher icon only. `web/favicon.png` and `web/icons/` are still the Flutter defaults.
- **MVP draft generator still paints a placeholder app icon.** `scripts/generate_mvp_drafts.py` writes `ui/app_icon_1024_draft.png`. Re-running it would replace the locked clash bytes.
- **Walk and throw are single frames.** No multi-frame cycles yet (`docs/STATUS.md`).
- **End-of-run ads use Google's sample IDs.** An interstitial can show at the end of a run, after the coin beat and before the defeat summary, and when Pause returns to the menu. It never shows during the fight, and a wave-clear shop does not offer one. Another show waits until 3 minutes of wall-clock time have passed since the last interstitial that actually displayed. That timer is for the app session and is not saved. Real AdMob IDs are not in the repo. iOS has the test app ID, SKAdNetworkItems, UMP, ATT, and a privacy manifest. Android has the test app ID only. Remove Ads is the non-consumable `dev.gamelogic.backyardbarrage.removeads`. The product still has to be created in App Store Connect, and the Paid Apps agreement still has to be signed. A refund does not clear the on-device entitlement. The Play Billing product is not set up.
- **CI checks format only on `lib/game` and `lib/seasons`.** Other folders have pre-existing format drift (for example `lib/meta/skill_tree.dart`). Widen the `dart format` step once they are formatted. Infos do not fail `flutter analyze`.
- **`flutter analyze` reports one pre-existing info.** `prefer_initializing_formals` in `lib/feel/feel_bus.dart`. It is already on `main` and is not part of the icon change.
- **Draft art otherwise.** Other PNGs are still `*_draft.png` until approved finals replace them (`docs/STATUS.md`). The app icon final name is the exception above.
- **README was art-pack oriented.** Rewritten as a game README in the agent-docs bootstrap; keep it game-focused if you touch it again.
